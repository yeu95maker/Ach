# MealMatch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

MealMatch is a recipe app. It is built to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

## What we collect
Nothing. MealMatch has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your name (if you enter one), preferences (diet, allergies, goals), filters, saved recipes, swipes and ratings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Internet use
The built-in recipes and photos are included in the app and work offline. If optional online recipe sources are switched on in a future version, the app only downloads recipes from them; it does not send them your personal information.

## Children
MealMatch does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Health information
Nutrition values are estimates for general guidance and are not medical advice.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/yeu95maker/ach or contact the developer through the App Store listing.
