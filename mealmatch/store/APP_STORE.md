## Description (max 4,000)
Not sure what to cook? MealMatch turns finding your next meal into a quick swipe.

Tell MealMatch what you like and what you need – your goals, your diet, your allergies – and it shows you recipes that fit. Swipe right to save, left to skip. The more you swipe, the better your suggestions get.

OVER 1,200 RECIPES
• Quick weeknight dinners, breakfasts, soups and desserts
• Weight-loss bowls and high-protein meals
• German classics, Asian favourites, Mediterranean dishes and more
• Hundreds of recipes with mouth-watering photos

MADE FOR YOUR GOALS
• Healthy & balanced, weight loss, high protein, low carb, high fibre or quick & easy
• Your goals shape which recipes you see first

DIET & ALLERGY FILTERS
• Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, egg-free, halal and kosher
• Hide recipes with peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, eggs, gluten, soy, sesame, fish or shellfish
• Add your own ingredients to avoid, like mushrooms or coriander

NUTRITION AT A GLANCE
• Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar and salt for every recipe
• Optional heart-, kidney-, liver- and diabetes-friendly filters based on simple nutrition limits

SIMPLE TO COOK
• Clear step-by-step methods – tap each step as you go
• Change the number of servings and the ingredients update
• Tips for swaps and making recipes your own

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your preferences and saved recipes stay on your phone
• Works offline

Please note: MealMatch is for general information only and is not medical advice. Nutrition values are estimates. Allergen labels come from the ingredient list – always check product labels. If you have a medical condition, talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian.

Some recipes are adapted from the Wikibooks Cookbook (CC BY-SA 4.0); each one links to its source.
