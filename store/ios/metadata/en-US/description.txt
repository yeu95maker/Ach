Dienstwerk is the rostering app for hospitals, rehab clinics and care homes. Nurses see their roster, swap shifts, enter wishes and request leave. Ward leads plan, approve and see at a glance how the ward is staffed.

Please note: Dienstwerk requires an account from your employer. Your organisation invites you; there is no public sign-up.

FOR NURSES
• Roster by month, week and day, with shift times and the time of the last change
• Shift swap: swap or hand over a shift with a colleague – they agree, the ward lead approves
• Open shifts: only colleagues who are free and keep the rest period are asked
• Who's on duty: who is working with you today
• Ward chat with the whole team of your ward
• Wish plan: enter preferred shifts and days off for the next roster
• Request leave, report sick in a few steps
• Read documents & SOPs and confirm with “Read and understood”
• Mandatory trainings: see your status, report participation, get reminded in time
• News: announcements, trainings, team meetings and workshops
• Notifications about new rosters, changes and decisions

FOR WARD LEADS
• Ward status: planned vs. target per shift, sick notes, leave and open shifts
• Roster editor for the whole team, with hints on wishes and rest periods
• Find cover and confirm volunteers with one tap
• Review and approve shift swaps – with hints on rest periods and wishes
• Publish SOPs and guidelines, track read confirmations, remind those missing
• Define mandatory trainings and see the team's status in one overview
• Approve leave, acknowledge sick notes, send announcements
• Import a finished roster from CSV or Excel (XLSX) – with checks and preview

FOR ADMINISTRATION AND IT
• Invite staff, assign roles and wards
• An interface your IT can use to send published rosters automatically; changes only apply after the ward lead confirms them
• Permissions per role; several organisations stay strictly separated

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking
• Colleagues only see the period of a sick note, never the reason
• Training certificates are visible only to you and your ward lead
• Your sign-in is kept in your device's secure storage

Dienstwerk is available in German, English, Turkish, Romanian and Polish, with light and dark design.
