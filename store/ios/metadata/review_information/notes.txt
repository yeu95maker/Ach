Pulshift is a business app for hospitals and care facilities (rostering, leave, sick notes, ward chat, team news). Accounts are created by the employer; there is no public sign-up, so please use the demo accounts below. All names and data in the demo are fictional.

Where to look
- Dienstplan / Roster: own shifts, open shifts ("Offene Dienste"), history
- Team: ward chat ("Stationschat", one conversation per ward), leave requests, sick notes, "Vertretung finden" (find cover)
- Profil → Verwaltung (administration), Profil → Datenschutz (privacy policy) and Hilfe & Kontakt (support, account deletion)
- The app language can be changed under Profil → App-Sprache (German, English, Turkish, Romanian, Polish).

Accounts and sign-in: users only sign in with accounts provided by their employer (guideline 4.8 exception for enterprise accounts). This review build offers e-mail and password only.
Account deletion: requested via Profil → Hilfe & Kontakt; the employer can also deactivate an account immediately.
Push notifications are optional and only contain a generic text without health data.
