Dienstwerk is a business app for hospitals and care facilities (rostering, shift swaps, leave, sick notes, ward chat, documents/SOPs, mandatory trainings, team news). Accounts are created by the employer; there is no public sign-up, so please use the demo accounts below. All names and data in the demo are fictional.

Where to look
- Home: today's shift, ward status (staffing per shift), "Zu erledigen" (documents to confirm, trainings due)
- Dienstplan / Roster: own shifts; tap a future shift → "Dienst tauschen" (shift swap). "Offene Dienste" (open shifts), "Diensttausch" (swap requests), history
- Swap flow: Anna requests a swap with Jonas → Jonas accepts under Dienstplan → Diensttausch → Mira approves there
- Team: ward chat ("Stationschat"), "Dokumente & SOPs" (read confirmation), "Pflichtfortbildungen" (mandatory trainings), leave requests, sick notes
- Profil → Verwaltung (administration), Profil → Datenschutz (privacy policy) and Hilfe & Kontakt (support, account deletion)
- The app language can be changed under Profil → App-Sprache (German, English, Turkish, Romanian, Polish).

Accounts and sign-in: users only sign in with accounts provided by their employer. This review build offers e-mail and password only.
Account deletion: Profil → Hilfe & Kontakt → request account deletion in the app; the employer can also deactivate an account immediately.
Push notifications are optional and only contain a generic title without health data.
File uploads (documents, training certificates) use the system file picker; no camera or photo library permission is requested.
