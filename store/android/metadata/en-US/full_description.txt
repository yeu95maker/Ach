ShiftCare is the rostering app for hospitals and care facilities. Nurses see their roster, enter shift wishes and request leave. Ward leads plan, approve and quickly find cover.

Please note: you need an account from your facility to use ShiftCare. Your facility invites you; there is no public sign-up.

FOR NURSES
• Your roster in month and list view, with shift times and when it was last updated
• Who's on duty: see who is working with you today
• Ward chat: message your whole ward team, e.g. to swap shifts
• Wish plan: enter preferred shifts and days off for the next roster
• See open shifts and register your interest
• Request leave and follow its status
• Report sick in a few steps
• News: announcements, training, team meetings and workshops
• Roster history going back years
• Notifications for new rosters, changes and decisions

FOR WARD LEADS
• Roster editor for the whole team, with hints about wishes and rest periods
• Review wish plans
• Approve leave, acknowledge sick notes
• Find cover: who is free, keeps the rest period and has shown interest
• Post and assign open shifts
• Send announcements to your team
• Bring in a finished roster from CSV or Excel (XLSX) files – checked and previewed before it is published

FOR ADMINISTRATORS
• Invite staff, assign roles and wards
• Manage shift types and organisation
• Permissions per role; multiple facilities stay strictly separated

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking
• Colleagues only see the period of a sick note, never the reason
• Sick-note comments are additionally encrypted
• Your sign-in is kept in your device's secure storage

ShiftCare is available in German, English, Turkish, Romanian and Polish, with light and dark appearance.
